About

Software, hardware, and the control loop between them.

I’m Anirudh, a Robotics Engineering graduate student at Worcester Polytechnic Institute. My background combines production embedded development with graduate robotics research.

I’m most interested in the hard boundary where software meets the physical world—sensors with noise, actuators with limits, and dynamic systems that refuse to behave like the simulation.
